Japan job starter list 🇯🇵

Everything I've shared on getting a job in Japan, in one place: recruiting agencies, job portals and job fairs. Save this page and share it with a friend who's planning the move.

Before you start

  • Recruiting agencies are free for you. The company that hires you pays them. If anyone asks you for money to "get you a job in Japan", it's a scam 🚨
  • Do you need Japanese? For many developer roles, not at first. Product, project, sales and translator roles do need it (N2–N1).
  • Not a techie? If your English is good, look at the JET Programme below.

Job fairs & programmes

  • Tokyo Job Fair, IndiaFree By the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. Online on Zoom: 28–29 Nov 2026. In person in New Delhi: 13 Feb 2027. For undergrads, new grads and professionals living in India; basic daily-conversation Japanese. Register for the online fair by 29 Nov.
  • JETRO Global Career ConnectOnline · English Online job fair in English, 1–2 Dec 2026, around 50 companies (IT, manufacturing, startups).
  • JET ProgrammeGood English Run by the Japanese government. ALT (assistant language teacher): good English, no Japanese needed; the 2027 ALT deadline in India is 30 Oct 2026. CIR (international relations): English and Japanese; it usually opens in January.
